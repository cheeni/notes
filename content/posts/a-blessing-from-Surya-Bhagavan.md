**A blessing from Surya Bhagavan.**

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Those who claim to have seen aliens or promise untold wealth through crypto coins and NFT sound more credulous than those who claim to have discovered their inherent divinity and surrendered to it. That's the world we live in today. We are so lost in materiality that we can't see a life without it.

&nbsp;

We are in the midst of WW3 even if the news won't announce it. There's a climate calamity that is unstoppable with an upper bound of a hundred years, if we are lucky. The future of the world is staked on an as yet non-existent AI super intelligence in a desperate hope that provided such a super intelligence won't kill all humans it will save them from their centuries of follies. The can of consequences has been kicked down the road for long enough, every government in the world is running out of options, their karmic bills are coming due. There's no way to provide for the survival of the entirety of humanity for the next hundred years, let alone their collective aspirations. The doomsday bunkers and self contained panic islands reveal the cards of the billionaire class.

&nbsp;

In these times of assured destruction people are still looking to go long on defense stocks, hedge against a currency collapse with gold and continue to trust in the power of their vote  in an attempt to buy insurance from entities whose own survival is in question.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

The world today runs on fear and greed and the rest of the seven deadly sins. Humanity has been losing its way for at least 4000 years, ever since the logic of logicians convinced man of the superiority of his intellect.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

The divine wish is to see a world that runs on unconditional love and a total unity of all life, where every human can feel the hunger pangs of the bear in the woods as their own because they are one consciousness, and will go out seeking to feed it, or even to offer this body as a meal, for such will be their generosity. Life exists on unconditional generosity and sacrifice, and cannot run on greed and selfishness.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

The goal of organized religion has been to keep the truth of God away from humans. It's catered to the petty desires for so long that they have become only about worldly power, so for sure institutional religions won't save humanity this time.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

In unprecedented times the rational voice is not one that sounds familiar and sane, but one that sounds like it has gone too far.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

I'm not here to be a prophet of doom but I'm tired of seeing good humans too busy “succeeding” in the current system to see it's upon its expiry.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

There's a divine government that maintains life that's invisible to the eyes of the human. There has always been, but one must surrender to it to receive its protection.

&nbsp;

One must die to one's narrow identity as an individual, as oneself, to be born into something bigger.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

This is not a journey that an institution, or guide can make for you, it is an intensely personal seeking. There isn't time to do this tomorrow or the day after, it might already be too late.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Despair and panic are not unreasonable reactions, but they form the basis of visada yoga, or the yoga of despair that can lead one into new knowledge because the old ways are thrown out as useless.

&nbsp;

Such a transformation cannot be engineered from above, it has to happen one consciousness at a time. For that is free will.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

God doesn't want anyone's surrender out of coercion or greed, it has to be unconditional and complete for no reason other than love.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

\---

&nbsp;

Just as a badly behaved child thinks the parent is only good as a walking ATM, the badly behaved human thinks gods and nature are only here for their convenience and that they have no responsibilities of behaviour or conduct.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

The temples of old were built in times when humans knew to respect life. When humans knew to sacrifice their surplus for the general well being.

&nbsp;

Today mountains are blown up to extract minerals, forests are denuded to rob their timber, lakes and rivers are emptied for greed. How can such a human treat the gods in temples any differently?&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Nala merely forgets to wash his feet, and Kali enters his being to possess him, making him lose his kingdom and go into exile. The actions of the modern human are far worse.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Just as Nala regains his true form only after trials and a surrender to truth, humans must own up to their follies and choose truth.
